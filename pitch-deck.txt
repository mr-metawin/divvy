EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Divvy is an immutable, trustless payment splitter for native BOT on the BOT
blockchain ecosystem. Built with Solidity ^0.8.24 and OpenZeppelin 5.x security
primitives, Divvy solves group revenue sharing and shared expenses by automating
the distribution of native crypto with mathematical precision.

Anyone can instantiate a split by choosing a name (1–50 bytes) and listing 2 to 20
member wallet addresses with basis point percentages summing to exactly 10,000 (100%).
Once deployed, the split is permanent and unalterable. Payer entities—clients, ticket
buyers, roommates, or automated smart contracts—simply send BOT to the split.
Each member can withdraw their exact proportional cut whenever they choose, with
zero counterparty risk and zero administrative overhead.

SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM - GROUP PAYMENT FRICTION IN WEB3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sharing revenue or splitting expenses among teams and collaborative groups remains
one of the most error-prone and contentious interactions in crypto:

1. Manual Accounting & "Chasing Down Money":
   - Freelance teams, bands, roommates, and hackathon squads frequently designate
     one person to receive payment and manually distribute shares.
   - Leads to human accounting errors, transaction fee disputes, and social awkwardness.

2. Custodial & Centralized Spreadsheets:
   - Off-chain spreadsheets and centralized tools require high trust in a single
     treasurer who could disappear, lose access, or misuse group funds.

3. Multi-Sig Complexity for Simple Splits:
   - Setting up a multi-signature safe requires coordinated signing for every routine
     payment, creating high gas overhead and operational bottlenecks for recurring revenue.

4. Push-Payment Failures (Smart Contract Denial of Service):
   - Traditional payment splitter contracts loop through all recipients in a single push.
   - If one recipient wallet rejects BOT (e.g. smart contract without a fallback),
     the entire payout transaction reverts, freezing everyone's money.

SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION - DIVVY PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Divvy introduces a frictionless, trustless protocol for group payouts:

1. Create in Seconds:
   - Define a split name, add 2 to 20 wallet addresses, and set percentage allocations.
   - Integrated "⚡ Split Equally" algorithm auto-fills equal shares and handles
     fractional basis point remainder on row 1 for an exact 100.00% sum.

2. Decentralized Native Inflow:
   - Anyone can send native BOT to `pay(id)` at any time in any increment.
   - Funds accumulate safely inside the contract's verified balance.

3. Asynchronous Pull Withdrawals:
   - Members withdraw their share whenever they want via `withdraw(id)`.
   - Each member's entitlement is calculated dynamically:
     `owed = (totalReceived * bps) / 10_000 - alreadyReleased`.
   - Each withdrawal is an isolated transaction. A malicious or broken contract
     only breaks its own withdrawal and can never block anyone else.

4. Trust-Minimized & Permanent:
   - No owner, no admin keys, no pause button, and no upgrade proxies.
   - Splits cannot be tampered with or modified after creation.

SLIDE 4: ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Divvy follows modern clean-architecture principles across contract and frontend:

A. SMART CONTRACT (contracts/src/Divvy.sol):
   - OpenZeppelin ReentrancyGuard: nonReentrant modifiers on `pay` and `withdraw`.
   - Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI): released state updated prior to low-level transfer.
   - Basis Point Storage: uint16 basis points (1 bp = 0.01%, 10,000 bps = 100.00%).
   - Direct Transfer Protection: No receive() or fallback(), preventing accidental loss.
   - Pure Custom Errors: 12 descriptive errors replacing verbose revert strings.

B. DATA MODEL:
   - struct Split {
       address creator;
       string name;
       uint256 totalReceived;
       uint64 createdAt;
     }
   - mapping(uint256 => Split) private _splits;
   - mapping(uint256 => address[]) private _splitMembers;
   - mapping(uint256 => mapping(address => uint16)) private _bps;
   - mapping(uint256 => mapping(address => uint256)) private _released;
   - mapping(address => uint256[]) private _splitIdsByMember;

C. DAPP FRONTEND (frontend/):
   - Vite + React 18 + TypeScript + Viem.
   - Client-side hash routing:
     * Home View (`#/`): Create split, auto-balancer, live total indicator, My Splits.
     * Split View (`#/split/<id>`): Stat cards, member table, payment box, 1-click withdraw.
   - Automatic RPC polling every 4 seconds for instant on-chain synchronization.

SLIDE 5: MATHEMATICAL PRECISION & DUST INTEGRITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Integer division in Solidity (`/ 10_000`) naturally truncates fractional wei:

1. Rounding Philosophy:
   - To prevent over-disbursing contract funds, each calculation rounds down:
     `entitled = (totalReceived * bps) / 10_000`.
   - Rounding down guarantees total payouts across all members NEVER exceed totalReceived.

2. Dust Bound Guarantee:
   - The residual wei left in the contract is mathematically proven to be strictly
     less than the number of members in the split:
     `dust < memberCount` (where memberCount <= 20).
   - For example, with 3 members splitting 7 wei unevenly (33.33%, 33.33%, 33.34%),
     dust left behind is 1 wei.

3. Stateful Invariant Verification:
   - Contract balance strictly matches:
     `address(divvy).balance == sum(split.totalReceived) - sum(all released)`.
   - Proved with 640 randomized handler calls across multiple actors without deviation.
