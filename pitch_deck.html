The Market Friction

The Problem: Group Payouts in Web3 are Broken

Collaborative squads, hackathon teams, bands, and roommates face high friction and trust barriers when handling shared income.

🧮
Manual Math & Spreadsheets
Designating a single "treasurer" to calculate splits and manually distribute crypto results in human errors, calculation disputes, and awkward social follow-ups.
🚨
Centralized Custodial Risk
Entrusting group funds to a single personal wallet introduces severe single-point-of-failure risks: loss of keys, exit scams, delays, or unauthorized spending.
🔐
Multi-Sig Overhead
Coordinating multi-signature safes for simple revenue sharing requires high operational friction and gas costs, requiring multi-signer quorum for every routine payout.
💥
Push-Payment DoS Vulnerability
Traditional splitter contracts iterate through all members in a single push loop. If one wallet rejects BOT, the whole transaction reverts, freezing everyone's cut.

Protocol Solution

The Solution: Trustless, Asynchronous Payment Splitting

Divvy replaces manual accounting and multi-sig bureaucracy with a pure, immutable smart contract.

⚡
1. Instant Immutable Split
Name the split (1–50 bytes) and list 2 to 20 member addresses with basis point percentages summing to exactly 10,000 (100.00%). Once created, splits are permanently fixed onchain.
⚡ Split Equally Auto-Balancing
📥
2. Permissionless Native Inflow
Clients, sponsors, or smart contracts simply call pay(id) with native BOT. Funds accumulate securely inside the contract's verified pool with zero protocol fee deductions.
Any Payer • Any Increment
💰
3. Isolated Pull Withdrawals
Each member independently withdraws their entitlement at any time. Owed amounts update dynamically across multiple payments. If a recipient rejects BOT, only their transaction fails.
Zero Griefing • Strict CEI

Engineering & Security

Smart Contract Architecture & Clean Primitives

Built with Solidity ^0.8.24 and OpenZeppelin 5.x with rigorous Checks-Effects-Interactions guarantees.

Robust Onchain Security Design
  • 🔒 OpenZeppelin ReentrancyGuard: nonReentrant modifiers protect both pay() and withdraw() entrypoints.
  • 🛡️ Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI): _released[id][msg.sender] increments BEFORE low-level external transfers.
  • 🚫 No receive() / fallback(): Direct plain transfers revert, ensuring all deposits route through pay(id) with explicit accounting.
  • 🛑 Pure Custom Errors: 12 bespoke custom errors replace heavy revert strings, significantly reducing bytecode size and gas costs.
  • 📜 Zero Admin Privilege: No contract owner, no upgrade proxy, no pause switch, and no percentage modification.
Mathematical Precision & Dust Bound
Solidity integer division truncates down, ensuring the contract never pays out more than total received:
uint256 totalEntitled = (totalReceived * uint256(bps)) / 10_000;
uint256 owed = totalEntitled - alreadyReleased;
Proven Invariant: The dust left behind in the contract is mathematically bounded to be strictly less than the number of members:
Dust ≤ (Member Count - 1) wei < 20 wei
